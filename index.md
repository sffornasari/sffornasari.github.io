# Simone Francesco Fornasari

<p class="lede">PostDoc in seismology, <a href="https://seisram.units.it">SeisRaM group</a>, University of Trieste.</p>

I work on real-time seismic monitoring and earthquake hazard assessment: earthquake early warning at near-fault observatories (NITRO), harmonized ground-shaking maps across institutes and borders (CONCORDIA), Bayesian spectral decomposition and site-response estimation (Campi Flegrei, Trieste), and seismic-noise methods for landslide characterization.
